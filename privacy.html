Privacy summary

Draft. Plain-language summary pending attorney review. It may change before ProStack opens to the public.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password (the password is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider).
  • A credit score range (not an exact score), counts of recent new accounts and Amex cards, and whether you bank with Bank of America or U.S. Bank.
  • Your business age and, if you choose, your business name.
  • The cards you track, with status, dates, credit limit and any denial reason you enter.
  • A repayment plan (amount and months) if you save one.
  • A record of the consents you accept and when.

What we never collect

Social Security numbers, full credit reports, card numbers, bank account numbers, or logins to any issuer or bank.

How it is used

To build and show your plan and tracker. Staff see only combined statistics, and only for groups of five or more decisions, to improve estimates. We do not sell your personal information. [Any change, including sharing with issuers, will be disclosed here and require your consent.]

Where it is stored and who can see it

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Each client's records are visible only to that client, enforced in the database. The portal is hosted on GitHub Pages, which serves the pages but does not receive your entries.

Your choices

  • Download everything we hold about you, or delete your profile, tracker and plans, from the Account tab.
  • To close your login completely, contact us at the address below. We keep the record of which consents you accepted.

Children

ProStack is for business owners aged 18 and over.
