What ProStack is

ProStack is a planning tool for business owners. You enter ranges and counts about your credit and your business, and it suggests which business credit cards may fit and a sensible order for applying.

What ProStack is not

  • It does not apply for credit for you, and it never asks for or stores your Social Security number, full credit report, card numbers, bank account numbers or issuer logins.
  • It does not guarantee or predict approval, credit limits or interest rates. Likelihood labels are estimates based on publicly reported issuer behavior and can be wrong.
  • It is not credit repair, a lender, or legal, tax or financial advice.

Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate information, and apply only for credit you intend to use and can repay.
  • Confirm every card term with the issuer before you apply. Terms change.
  • You apply directly with each issuer and are responsible for your own accounts and payments.
